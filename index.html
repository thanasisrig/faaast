A fast, start to finish

01

Pick your fast and start.

16:8 means 16 hours without food and an 8-hour window to eat. Choose that, 18:6, 20:4, one meal a day, 36 hours or your own length. Started late? You can change the start time afterwards.

02

Every hour fills a block.

The clock still counts the minutes, but the blocks show the bigger picture: 13 done, 3 to go. It’s easier to keep going when you can see it.

03 PRO

Know what’s happening in your body.

Faaast shows the stage you’re likely in, from digesting your last meal to burning fat to ketosis, and lets you know when you reach the next one. Log water and electrolytes as you go.

04

Go past your goal and the flames come out.

Every hour after your goal shows up as a flame block. It’s a small touch, but it’s surprisingly hard to stop at one.

05

End the fast, mark the day.

Tap End fast and the day fills in on your calendar. Fast on days in a row and they join into a streak.

Questions

What does Faaast do?

It times your fasts. Start one when you finish eating, watch the hours fill, and end it at your next meal. Each fast goes on a calendar, so over the weeks you can see your streaks, your records and how you’re getting on. You can track your weight and take progress photos in the same app.

Is it free?

Yes. Timing fasts, the calendar, records, weight, progress photos, widgets and all 62 badges are free, with no time limit. Faaast Pro adds fasting stages, water and electrolytes, a weight chart, Apple Health, iCloud backup and three more timer styles.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start, there’s nothing to sign up to. Your fasts, weight and photos stay on your iPhone, and go to your own iCloud only if you turn on backup.

What do 16:8 and OMAD mean?

They describe how long you fast. 16:8 is 16 hours without food and an 8-hour window to eat. 18:6 and 20:4 work the same way. OMAD means one meal a day, which is about 23 hours of fasting. Faaast also has 12:12, 14:10, 36 hours, or any length you set.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Export your fasts from your old app as a CSV or JSON file, then import them in Faaast from Settings → Your data. You can export everything from the same place.

Can I cancel Pro any time?

Yes. Go to your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-off purchase, so there’s nothing to cancel.

Is this medical advice?

No. Fasting stages are typical timings, and everyone is different. Don’t fast without medical advice if you’re pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have had an eating disorder, or take insulin or other glucose-lowering medication.
