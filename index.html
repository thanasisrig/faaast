Questions

What does Faaast actually do?

It times your fasts. Start one in a tap, watch the hours fill on the timer style you like, and end it when you eat. Every fast lands on a calendar, so you can see your streaks, records and progress over weeks and months. Weight, progress photos and achievements sit alongside.

Is it free?

Yes. Fasting, the calendar, records, weight, progress photos, widgets and all 62 achievements are free. Faaast Pro adds fasting stages, water and electrolytes, iCloud backup, Apple Health, a weight chart and three extra timer styles.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no server and no analytics. Everything stays on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud only if you turn on backup.

Which fasts does it support?

16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD, 12:12, 14:10, 36 hours, or any length you choose.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Import fasts from a CSV or JSON file, and export everything any time from Settings → Your data.

Can I cancel Pro any time?

Yes. Manage or cancel in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-off purchase with nothing to cancel.

Is this medical advice?

No. Fasting stages are typical timings and vary from person to person. Don’t fast without medical advice if you’re pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have had an eating disorder, or take insulin or other glucose-lowering medication.
