Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email hi@arecon.eu and we’ll get back to you.

  • Restore Faaast Pro on a new iPhone: Settings → Faaast Pro → Restore purchases.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Faaast.
  • Your data: Settings → Your data, to export it or delete everything.
