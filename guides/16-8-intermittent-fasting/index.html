16:8 intermittent fasting, explained

Updated 22 October 2026 · By the Faaast team

16:8 is the most popular way to do intermittent fasting. You go 16 hours without food and eat within the other 8. It sounds hard, but most of those 16 hours happen while you’re asleep.

What 16:8 means

The two numbers are hours. 16 is how long you fast. 8 is your eating window. If you finish dinner at 8 pm and eat again at noon the next day, that’s 16:8.

You don’t eat less food than usual on purpose. You just eat it within a shorter part of the day.

A sample day

TimeWhat happens
12:00First meal. Your eating window opens.
16:00A snack, if you want one.
19:30Dinner, your last meal of the day.
20:00Your eating window closes and the fast starts.
23:00 – 07:00Sleep. That’s 8 of your 16 hours done.
MorningWater, black coffee or plain tea.
12:0016 hours. The fast ends with your first meal.

The times aren’t fixed. If your mornings start early, try eating from 9 am to 5 pm instead. What matters is keeping roughly the same times each day.

What you can drink while fasting

  • Yes: water, sparkling water, black coffee, plain tea and herbal tea.
  • No: anything with calories. Milk in your coffee, juice, sugar and smoothies all end the fast.
  • It depends: diet drinks and sweeteners have almost no calories, but some people find they make them hungrier. Try with and without.

How to start without struggling

Going straight to 16 hours is the main reason people give up in the first week. Build up instead:

  1. Week 1: 12:12. Stop eating after dinner and have breakfast 12 hours later. For many people that’s barely a change.
  2. Week 2: 14:10. Push breakfast back by two hours.
  3. Week 3: 16:8. Push it back two more, or finish dinner a bit earlier.

Hunger usually comes in waves and passes in 15 to 20 minutes. A glass of water or a coffee helps it pass.

Common mistakes

  • Making up for it in the window. 16:8 works best when your meals stay normal-sized. Fasting doesn’t cancel out a huge dinner.
  • Not drinking enough. A lot of what feels like hunger in the morning is thirst.
  • Changing the times every day. Your body gets used to a rhythm. A different window every day makes it harder.
  • Being too strict. A birthday dinner that runs late isn’t a failure. Start the next fast when you finish eating and carry on.

Questions

Does sleep count towards the 16 hours?

Yes. Any time you’re not eating counts, so most people do half their fast or more asleep.

Can I drink coffee during 16:8?

Yes, as long as it’s black. Milk, cream and sugar have calories and end the fast.

Do I have to do 16:8 every day?

No. Many people fast every day because it’s easier to keep a routine, but some fast five days a week and eat normally at weekends. Pick what you can keep doing.

Will 16:8 help me lose weight?

It can help, because a shorter eating window often means eating less overall. It isn’t magic though: what and how much you eat in your window still matters most.

What if 16 hours is too hard?

Start with 12:12 or 14:10 and build up a couple of hours a week. Faaast has 12:12, 14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4 and one meal a day, so you can move up when you’re ready.
